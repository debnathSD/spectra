# Code of Conduct

Spectra's community follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. In short:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Spectra to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect different views and experiences, and give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use and build Spectra.

## What isn't acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their permission.
- Anything else that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all of Spectra's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when representing the
project elsewhere. Report anything unacceptable to the maintainer, [@debnathSD](https://github.com/debnathSD), by the
contact on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or
reject contributions, and warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, following
the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
